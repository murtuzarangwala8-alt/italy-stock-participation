Key Takeaway

Stock market participation in Italy is strongly segmented by socioeconomic status. Higher income raises participation because richer households have more savings after consumption. Higher education raises participation through financial literacy, information access, and confidence in investment products.

The findings point to an underserved group: households with moderate-to-high income but lower education. These households may have sufficient resources to invest but face informational barriers or low financial confidence. Simplified investment products and targeted financial education could meaningfully increase participation among this group.
